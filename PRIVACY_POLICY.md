# Privacy Policy for YouTube Focus AI

**Effective Date:** December 14, 2025

## 1. Introduction
YouTube Focus AI ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
Our extension prioritizes your privacy. We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide our services.

### A. Personal Data
*   **We do NOT collect personally identifiable information** (PII) such as your name, email, or address.
*   **API Keys:** Your MiniMax API Key is stored **locally** on your device using Chrome's secure storage API (`chrome.storage.local`). It is never sent to our servers; it is only used to communicate directly with the MiniMax API provider to generate learning content.

### B. Browsing Data
*   **Video Titles:** To provide the "Distraction Guard" feature, the extension temporarily reads the title of the YouTube video link you click. This text is sent to the AI API to determine relevance.
*   **Relevance Check:** This data is ephemeral and is not permanently stored by us.
*   **Watch History:** A local history of your watched videos (title and relevance status) is stored **on your device** to help prevent "rabbit holes." This data does not leave your browser.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party service:
*   **MiniMax API:** We use this service to generate course curriculums, quizzes, and analyze video relevance. Please refer to [MiniMax's Privacy Policy](https://api.minimax.io/) for details on how they handle data sent to their API.

## 4. Data Security
All data (Course progress, API keys, Watch history) is stored locally within your browser instance. We do not maintain external servers or databases for user data.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Chrome Web Store support page.
